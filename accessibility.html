ADA & accessibility

Accessible water information for every New Mexican.

HYDROS is designed as a public-service prototype with accessibility, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, semantic structure, language access, and alternate-format support built into the architecture.

Accessibility statement

HYDROS aims to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA and applicable Section 508 accessibility principles for public digital services. Production release requires formal accessibility testing, remediation, documented conformance review, and agency approval.

Users who cannot access content or a service should be offered an equivalent method of access and a clear pathway to request a reasonable accommodation or alternate format.

Report a barrier

A production service should collect the page URL, a short description of the barrier, assistive technology if the user chooses to provide it, preferred contact method, and urgency where a filing deadline or public meeting is involved.

Language access

English and Spanish first, expandable by need.

High-value public pages should use reviewed human translations, clear language switching, consistent navigation, translated vital documents where required, and a visible method for requesting language assistance.

Machine translation may assist drafting or discovery, but official public-language content should receive appropriate human review before publication.

Civil rights & nondiscrimination

Equal access to public water services.

HYDROS should provide nondiscriminatory access to programs and digital services consistent with applicable federal and New Mexico civil-rights requirements. Final production language, complaint procedures, responsible officials, deadlines, and contact information must be approved by the agency’s legal and civil-rights authorities.
