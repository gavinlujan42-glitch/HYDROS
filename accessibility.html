ACCESS FOR EVERY NEW MEXICAN

Design public water services to work for everyone.

The HYDROS/AquaTech prototype targets accessible navigation, keyboard operation, readable contrast, screen-reader structure, alternate formats, language access, and clear accommodation pathways.

ADA & digital accessibility

Production should target WCAG 2.2 AA and applicable Section 508-aligned practices, with formal testing and an approved accessibility statement.

Request an accommodation

Production should publish an agency-approved contact path for reasonable accommodations, auxiliary aids, accessible documents, and alternate formats.

Report a barrier

Provide a clear method to report inaccessible pages, documents, maps, forms, or media and track remediation.

Language access

High-value landing pages should have reviewed Spanish translations and clear interpretation/translation assistance routes.

Nondiscrimination & civil rights

Public services should provide nondiscriminatory access consistent with applicable federal and New Mexico requirements.

Official agency contacts

Use official OSE channels for current ADA, accommodation, language-access, or civil-rights contacts.

Prototype policy notice: Final production language, named coordinators, complaint procedures, and response timeframes require agency legal, ADA, civil-rights, PIO, and web-governance approval.
