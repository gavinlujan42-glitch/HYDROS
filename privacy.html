Privacy & security

Public service with clear data boundaries.

This prototype demonstrates the privacy, security, records, and payment principles that should be explicit before HYDROS becomes a production public system.

Privacy statement

Production HYDROS should identify what information is collected, the legal and business purpose for collection, whether submission is required or optional, how information is used, retention requirements, potential disclosure under public-record laws, and where users can ask questions.

Prototype rule: do not submit sensitive personal information to these demonstration forms.

Minimize collection

  • Collect only information needed for the public service.
  • Separate public records from restricted information.
  • Use role-based access and auditable administrative activity.
  • Publish retention and disposition rules.
  • Provide clear notices before collecting information.

Security

Protect the service and reduce payment scope.

Payments

Use an approved hosted PCI DSS payment provider so HYDROS does not store card numbers, CVV, or sensitive authentication data.

Identity

Use approved authentication, least privilege, strong administrative controls, session protection, and appropriate multifactor authentication.

Files

Scan uploads for malware, validate file types, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and apply records-management controls.

Monitoring

Log administrative actions, security events, API failures, and significant changes while avoiding unnecessary sensitive-data logging.

Website disclaimer

Know what is authoritative.

HYDROS prototype content is illustrative. It does not create, modify, adjudicate, transfer, approve, deny, or determine a water right or permit; establish an official filing date; calculate an authoritative fee; replace statutory notice; or provide legal advice. For official information, rely on approved OSE systems, official records, applicable statutes and rules, and authorized agency staff.

Before production: privacy, terms-of-use, security, records, ADA, language-access, payment, civil-rights, and disclaimer language should be reviewed and approved by the responsible agency officials and counsel.
