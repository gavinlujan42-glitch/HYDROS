TRUST BY DESIGN

Make data collection visible, limited, and understandable.

A production AquaTech privacy program should tell users what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, who receives it, and what protections apply.

Privacy notice

Production should document analytics, logs, cookies, form data, account information, geolocation, third-party services, retention, disclosure, and user rights where applicable.

Security notice

Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, payment-card data, protected health information, or other sensitive data to this prototype.

Payment separation

Card and ACH entry should occur only within an approved PCI DSS-compliant hosted payment environment, not inside HYDROS.

Public records & retention

Production workflows must align privacy notices with applicable records-retention schedules and New Mexico public-record requirements.

Website disclaimer

Prototype information is illustrative and is not an official permit, filing date, water-right determination, legal record, statutory notice, fee schedule, or legal advice.

Official public records

Use the official OSE public-record pathway for formal record requests.

Production requirement: final privacy, security, disclaimer, records, and terms language should be reviewed and approved by OSE legal, security, privacy, records, ADA, and PIO/web governance owners.
