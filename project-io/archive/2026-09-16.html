Keep political and water ledgers separate.

Published September 16, 2026 · 8:22 AM MDT

ORANGE · GOVERNANCE-PROVENANCE WATCH

A report identifies a $12,400 Oracle America contribution in Deb for New Mexico’s campaign filing. It belongs in the governance ledger, but it supplies no evidence about water-right administration, permit outcomes or improper influence.

Attributable record

The Candle reports an August 26 Oracle America contribution and reproduces the claimed filing entry. The official CFIS portal is the primary system; independent transaction extraction remains pending. The article’s broader $198,000 aggregate is not adopted without a recipient-level ledger.

NMED continues to identify the YGI air proceeding as stayed. No new OSE meter history, August 31 water report, pipeline approval or Supreme Court merits order was located.

What is not established

No evidence reviewed connects the reported campaign contribution to a water, air, IRB or infrastructure decision. A disclosed contribution is not proof of a quid pro quo or corruption. Haaland is not presently adjudicating Jupiter permits.

HYDROS judgment

Maintain two firewalled ledgers: campaign-finance transactions by contributor and recipient; water administration by right, permit, meter, purpose, place and date. Join them only when a document establishes a relevant communication or official act.

Required evidence

  1. Complete campaign filing and normalized contributor aliases.
  2. Recipient-level transaction export with amendments and refunds.
  3. OSE meter readings and the August 31 water report.
  4. Documented communications, if any, linking contributors to official actions.
