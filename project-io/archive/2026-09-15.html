ORANGE · PERMIT-BOUNDARY WATCH

Oracle says current data-center construction is separate from YGI’s adjacent microgrid and its stayed air-permit case. The new record also places an 11-million-gallon one-time non-potable fill and 168,000-gallon annual maintenance estimate in the applicant’s technical narrative.

Attributable record

Oracle states that county-authorized data-center construction is outside permit 10883 and that YGI has discussed microgrid preconstruction with NMED. NMED’s September 14 dashboard still listed the permit under review. Oracle also discloses that YGI retained Christopher Ollson, whose comments support the revised fuel-cell design and the cited water estimates.

What is not established

No written NMED concurrence, parcel-level activity log or inspection record was located. Oracle’s compliance assertion and the retained expert’s conclusions are not agency or court findings. The 11-million-gallon and 168,000-gallon figures address initial and operating systems, not construction water. No new OSE meter history, August 31 water report, pipeline approval or Supreme Court merits order was found. No evidence reviewed establishes corruption.

HYDROS judgment

Pair facility provenance with water provenance. Every withdrawal and every construction activity should map to a parcel, operator, permit, meter, purpose and date. Separate construction pumping, one-time system fills, annual maintenance and potable workforce use.

Required evidence

  1. Parcel map, ownership/control and site legal descriptions.
  2. County permits and contractor scopes for each facility.
  3. NMED preconstruction guidance, correspondence and inspections.
  4. Full retained-expert report and absolute emissions estimates.
  5. Metered construction water and documentation for the one-time fill.
