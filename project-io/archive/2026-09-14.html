ORANGE · GRID-BOUNDARY WATCH

Oracle’s current El Paso Electric service for construction and house/backup power is analytically different from full operational grid power. A newly indexed county-tape review also places an EPE easement in the public record, making its terms the next infrastructure document to obtain.

Attributable record

A timestamped civic review of the September 8 county tape records Oracle describing EPE construction and house/backup service and discussion of an EPE easement. No executed instrument was located. The proposed 2.45 GW Bloom system remains the primary operating design; full grid supply remains an undefined fallback tied to the air-permit outcome.

What is not established

Existing limited utility service does not prove that the future data-center load will operate from the public grid. No interconnection application, load study, rate filing or executed full-load agreement was located. No new OSE meter history, August 31 water report, pipeline approval or Supreme Court merits order was found. No evidence reviewed establishes corruption.

HYDROS judgment

Power and water need parallel provenance controls. For power: construction, backup, operations and renewable matching. For water: construction, initial fills, operations and potable workforce use. The EPE easement should disclose what facilities cross which land, for how long, at whose cost and for which load category.

Required evidence

  1. Executed or draft EPE easement and legal description.
  2. Service meters, peak demand, capacity limits and payer.
  3. Any full-load interconnection study or transmission upgrade plan.
  4. Agreement analysis distinguishing limited present service from an operating-grid switch.
  5. OSE meter readings and separate construction and operational water ledgers.
