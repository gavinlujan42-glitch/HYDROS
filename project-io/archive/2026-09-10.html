Water, land and infrastructure edition

The power plan expands; the water ledger does not.

Published September 10, 2026 · 8:49 AM MDT

ORANGE · UNCHANGED

Oracle’s 2-GW renewable-energy RFP is a material planning signal, but it does not resolve Jupiter’s gas-pipeline, air-permit or water-accounting disputes. County monitoring-committee appointments were deferred to September 22.

Verified developments

Oracle announced an RFP seeking 2 GW of new solar, wind, geothermal and other renewable generation in New Mexico, with target delivery from 2027 through 2031. Oracle associates the solicitation with carbon-free energy matching for Project Jupiter by 2031.

The September 8 county meeting did not produce the expected 11-member Community Monitoring Committee roster. Reporting says commissioners moved the appointments to September 22.

Oracle continues to condition its economic projections on approval of the project’s air permit and natural-gas pipeline as originally planned.

Water and infrastructure meaning

Renewable matching does not eliminate onsite gas use. Bloom fuel cells remain the proposed firm-generation source; the public RFP does not establish hourly matching, direct physical delivery, storage or transmission capacity.

No new public copy of Oracle’s August 31 acre-feet report or the underlying OSE totalizer ledger surfaced. Construction water, initial fills, operating cooling/power water and potable workforce use remain distinct categories.

No new pipeline right-of-way approval or Supreme Court merits order was located by publication time. No evidence reviewed today establishes corruption.
