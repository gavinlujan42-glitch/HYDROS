Secure transaction pathway

Know what you are paying for before you leave HYDROS.

The agency-facing system creates the order and identifies the service. An approved PCI DSS-compliant hosted processor handles card or ACH data. HYDROS receives only a transaction result, amount, timestamp, and receipt reference.

PCI scope minimized

3. Continue to approved hosted checkout

PCI boundary
  • HYDROS creates a signed transaction/order reference.
  • The browser redirects to the approved payment provider.
  • The provider collects card or ACH information inside its controlled environment.
  • The provider returns success/failure, amount, timestamp, and transaction token.
  • HYDROS stores no PAN, CVV, or sensitive authentication data.

4. Receipt and filing/service status

No payment simulated.

After success, the production receipt would show the amount, agency reference, processor confirmation, payment timestamp, payer email, and the filing/service satisfied.

What can be paid?

  • Application and permit filing fees
  • Well-related fees where authorized
  • Change, transfer, proof, extension, conveyance, protest, or specialty transaction fees
  • Invoices and other authorized public-service charges

Payment does not replace filing review

Where payment is required to complete a filing, HYDROS should not display Filed until the authoritative agency system confirms both acceptance and payment.
