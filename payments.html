Payment center

Pay water-service fees securely and know exactly what the payment applies to.

HYDROS separates agency services from card processing. The production design would create a payment order, transfer the user to an approved PCI DSS-compliant hosted payment provider, and receive only a tokenized transaction result and receipt metadata.

STEP 3

Continue to secure hosted payment

Production handoff
  • HYDROS creates a signed payment order and unique transaction reference.
  • The browser redirects to the approved external payment provider.
  • The provider handles card or ACH information within its PCI-controlled environment.
  • HYDROS receives a success/failure token, amount, timestamp, and receipt reference.
  • No primary account number, CVV, or sensitive authentication data is stored by HYDROS.
STEP 4

Receipt and filing status

No payment simulated yet.

After a successful hosted transaction, the receipt would show amount, reference, processor confirmation, date/time, and the filing or service it satisfies.

What can be paid here?

The production payment center could support agency-approved fee classes such as:

  • Water-right application filing fees
  • Change, transfer, or ownership-related filings
  • Well permit and related service fees
  • Proofs, extensions, protests, and specialized filings
  • Licensing or registration fees where authorized
  • Other invoiced OSE public services

Payment lifecycle

Order createdHosted checkoutToken returnedReceipt posted

Where payment is a legal prerequisite to filing, HYDROS would not display “Filed” until the authoritative agency system confirms payment and acceptance.

Need help?

If the amount looks wrong, a payment was duplicated, or you are unsure which filing the fee belongs to, contact the responsible OSE office before making another payment.
