Applications & permitting

Start the right water filing with confidence.

HYDROS guides applicants through filing type, district routing, required forms, supporting documents, certification, completeness review, payment, and receipt. The production service would connect directly to approved OSE workflows.

Application pathway

1. Choose a filing2. Prepare documents3. Applicant & contact4. Upload PDF5. Certify submission6. Tracking & review7. Pay required fees8. Receipt & confirmation

1Choose your filing type

Select the action closest to what you need. HYDROS would then identify the correct OSE form, district office, fee class, and supporting-document checklist.

Prototype route: Application to Appropriate → determine basin/district → prepare signed form and supporting documents → completeness review → fee payment.

2Prepare your documents

Completed application form
Use the approved current OSE form.
Signature / certification
Ensure required signatures are present.
Supporting exhibits
Maps, deeds, legal descriptions, notices, or technical materials as applicable.
Single PDF package

HYDROS separates received, under review, complete, and filed so applicants always know what the status means.

ReceivedEmail verifiedCompleteness reviewPayment dueFiled / receipt
Why this matters

A confirmation that documents were uploaded is not necessarily the legal filing date. The production workflow would display the authoritative status and timestamp returned by the official system.
